Attack Lab: paste anything untrusted

SIMULATED WORST-CASE AGENTLIVE

Type or paste a document, email or web page, in plain English or Hindi, Kannada or Hinglish, or hidden in base64 or invisible Unicode. The agent here is a simulated worst-case model: it obeys any instruction it finds in content. You see the same content replayed without and with AgentShield.

Injection mutation fuzzer

Six attack seeds (exfiltration, fraud, PII harvest, destruction, beaconing, internal phishing) across fifteen classes, from plain English to obfuscated encodings. Each variant runs twice, without and with AgentShield, and is judged from world state. Two classes, ROT13 and reversed text, are intentionally outside what AgentShield decodes. They show what still holds when detection is blind.

Self red-team loop

Runs the fuzzer and a benign corpus. For each class with a miss, it takes the smallest failing document as a reproduction, proposes a policy change, replays the whole campaign on a copy of the policy, and writes a regression test. Nothing goes live until a person accepts it.

Pending approvals

High-impact actions stop here. Nothing runs until a named person approves. Before running, the call is checked again against current policy and against the exact arguments that were requested.

Chain integrity

Each entry stores the hash of the previous one plus an HMAC. Editing, deleting or reordering any entry breaks verification at that exact point.

Tamper demonstration

Copies the log, rewrites one DENY decision to ALLOW the way an attacker covering their tracks would, then verifies the copy. The real log is not touched.
